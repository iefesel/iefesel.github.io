For a long time I wanted a corner of the internet that is my own, independent of social media. Today my site is finally live.

### Why a website?

Anything posted on social media disappears from the feed within a few days. On a website my writing stays organised, I can come back to it whenever I want, and I can share it with a single link.

### What will be here?

- Notes from physics courses and from what I read
- What I learn about particle physics and accelerators
- Small calculations and projects in Python and MATLAB

Thanks for reading.
